How Far is Adam from Natural Gradient Descent?

2026-10-03

Summary

This study analyzes how closely Adam, a standard optimization algorithm in deep learning, geometrically aligns with Natural Gradient Descent (NGD) and identifies the true factors behind its empirical success.

Key Points

  • The researchers analyzed Adam's full update rule, including momentum, from the perspective of a diagonal empirical Fisher information approximation and measured its geometric deviation from NGD across four loss landscapes.
  • While the deviation was small under well-conditioned settings, it reached approximately 10^3 in ill-conditioned settings and neural networks; however, geometric drift did not adversely affect final loss minimization, even if it slowed initial convergence.
  • The study identified that Adam's superior optimization performance stems not from closely tracking the NGD trajectory, but rather from a balance between structural approximation error and momentum smoothing.

SW-KAN: Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks with Stieltjes-Wigert q-Orthogonal Polynomials

2026-10-03

Summary

This study proposes SW-KAN, a novel Kolmogorov-Arnold Network architecture incorporating Stieltjes-Wigert q-orthogonal polynomials to resolve the domain mismatch between unbounded real inputs and orthogonal polynomial bases.

Key Points

  • It adopts Stieltjes-Wigert q-orthogonal polynomials defined on the semi-infinite interval (0, infinity) and utilizes a smooth exponential-of-tanh mapping to reliably bridge the domain gap while maintaining favorable gradient conditions.
  • Computational efficiency is achieved by utilizing a numerically stable three-term recurrence relation that evaluates polynomial expansions in O(N) operations without invoking special functions.
  • By introducing inductive bias through a log-normal weight structure and learnable q-parameters, it demonstrates superior expressiveness and accuracy-efficiency trade-offs over conventional polynomial KANs, even in resource-constrained settings such as reduced dimensionality or limited data.

What’s next after Jev? Metacache: Reasoning by Construction [D]

2026-10-03

Summary

This post introduces Metacache, an inference framework that shifts away from a single monolithic model approach toward constructing and reusing compound systems combining small agents with code orchestration.

Key Points

  • To address the unpredictability and difficulty of modifying monolithic models, the paradigm is shifting toward compound systems combining small agents with code-based orchestration layers.
  • Instead of having a model answer directly, it proposes a method where the model constructs and executes a compound system (reasonlet) composed of small agents, returning both the result and the system itself.
  • The returned reasonlet can be saved locally for repeated execution and modification without re-invoking the model, or cached on the provider side (metacache) to save computational resources.

[Marginalized SW Part 1] SW Disappears from Budget Proposal... Concerns Over National Competitiveness

2026-10-03

Summary

While the government significantly increases next year's AI budget, support budgets for the software (SW) industry—which serves as the core foundation for AI—have been reduced, raising concerns over weakened national competitiveness.

Key Points

  • The Ministry of Science and ICT's 2027 AI budget reached 9.4211 trillion KRW, up 84.3% from the previous year, whereas the non-R&D SW budget dropped by around 40 billion KRW to approximately 290 billion KRW.
  • Budgets for key SW projects, including SW infrastructure expansion, public SaaS development and transition support, and global expansion capability enhancement, were cut significantly, and university support focus is shifting from SW to AI.
  • Industry and academia emphasize that for AI technology to be applied in real-world industrial settings and generate value, support from the traditional SW ecosystem, such as ERP, DB, and embedded SW, is essential.
Notable Quotes & Details

Notable Data / Quotes

  • Out of the 29.6476 trillion KRW under the Ministry of Science and ICT's jurisdiction, 9.4211 trillion KRW was allocated to AI. This represents an 84.3% increase compared to this year's 5.0938 trillion KRW, accounting for 31.8% of the ministry's total budget.
  • Next year's non-R&D SW-related budget stands at around 290 billion KRW, down approximately 40 billion KRW from about 330 billion KRW this year.
  • Eo Yoon-ho, President of Korea Commercial AI Software Association: 'GPUs are ultimately infrastructure and models are foundational technologies...
