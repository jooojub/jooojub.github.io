How Far is Adam from Natural Gradient Descent?

2026-10-03

Summary

This study analyzes how geometrically aligned Adam, the standard optimization algorithm in deep learning, is with Natural Gradient Descent (NGD) and identifies the actual factors behind its success.

Key Points

  • Analyzed Adam's full update rule, including momentum, from the perspective of diagonal empirical Fisher information approximation and measured its geometric deviation from NGD across four loss landscapes.
  • While deviations were small under well-conditioned settings, they reached approximately 10^3 in ill-conditioned settings and neural networks, and geometric drift did not adversely affect final loss minimization even if it slowed initial convergence.
  • Demonstrated that Adam's superior optimization performance does not stem from closely tracking the NGD trajectory, but from the balance between structural approximation error and momentum smoothing.
