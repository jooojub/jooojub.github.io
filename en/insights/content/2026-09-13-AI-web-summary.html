Probabilistic Focal Search: Accelerating Bounded-Suboptimal Search via Lower-Bound Advancement

2026-09-12

Summary

This study introduces Probabilistic Focal Search (PFS), an algorithm that incorporates a probabilistic node expansion mechanism to address the issue of delayed lower-bound updates in bounded-suboptimal search.

Key Points

  • To overcome the limitation of conventional Focal Search (FS), where search is delayed due to stagnation of the lower bound (f_min), PFS proposes following the FS heuristic with probability p and expanding the minimum f node with probability 1-p to drive lower-bound advancement.
  • Benchmark experiments on domains such as N-Puzzle, Pancake Sorting, and TSP demonstrated performance improvements, reducing the number of node expansions by approximately 90% or more in environments with prolonged lower-bound stagnation intervals.
  • APFS, an anytime extension that can terminate at any point, outperformed existing algorithms in GCTSP environments, and the study also demonstrated the extensibility of applying the same mechanism to Dynamic Potential Search (PDPS).

Quantifying the Memorization-to-Generalization Transition: Scaling Laws and Phase Structure in Grokking

2026-09-12

Summary

A study quantitatively investigating the power-law scaling relations between hyperparameters and the timing of grokking—a phenomenon where neural networks transition from simple memorization to generalization—as well as its phase transition boundaries.

Key Points

  • By analyzing 384 configurations of two-hidden-layer MLPs on modular arithmetic tasks, the study derived power-law scaling laws for the onset of generalization (T_grok).
  • The primary driver of the generalization transition is data complexity (D) rather than model capacity (H); doubling the data accelerates generalization by approximately 4x, whereas doubling the hidden layer width results in only about a 1.2x speedup.
  • A clear phase boundary determining the occurrence of grokking was identified around weight decay λ ≳ 1.0, and monotonic compression of the weight norm was observed during the transition process.

City of San Francisco Demands Meta Stop AI-Generated Child Sexual Abuse Ads

2026-09-12

Summary

The City of San Francisco has demanded that Meta halt the placement of AI-generated child sexual abuse material ads across its platforms, including Facebook and Instagram, and provide an explanation.

Key Points

  • The San Francisco City Attorney sent a 4-page letter to Meta urging an immediate halt to AI-based child sexual abuse material (CSAM) ads and demanding an explanation for their repeated publication.
  • Investigations revealed that more than 350 ads for apps that convert images of minors into explicit sexual videos were posted on Facebook, Instagram, and Threads, reaching tens of thousands of accounts.
  • Meta stated that it removed the identified ads and claimed the matter fell outside local jurisdiction; however, counterarguments highlighted that hundreds of additional ads ran even after detection tools were implemented.
Notable Quotes & Details

Notable Data / Quotes

  • Ran more than 350 ads converting still images of minors into explicit sexual videos
  • Reached more than 29,000 accounts in the European Union (EU)
  • More than 250 additional ads were run even after Wired first reported on 53 ads in early August
  • A Meta spokesperson stated, 'We do not tolerate child exploitation, regardless of whether it involves nudify apps or real versus AI-generated content.'
