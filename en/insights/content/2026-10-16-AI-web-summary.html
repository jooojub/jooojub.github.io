Lotus: Learning to Understand Time-series by Learning to Forecast

2026-10-15

Summary

Proposes the principle of 'forecast grounding', which pre-trains time-series language models to maintain future-forecasting capabilities, alongside the LOTUS model that implements it.

Key Points
